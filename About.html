[ FOUNDER STORY ]

In 2024, after years of shipping AI features inside product teams at startups and enterprises, we kept seeing the same pattern: every business had a stack of tools, a backlog of manual processes, and no one technically equipped to wire them together properly.

The first wave of "AI agencies" filled the gap with brittle Zapier flows and chatbots glued to OpenAI. It worked, until it didn't. Tools changed, prompts drifted, no one knew why the system broke.

We started this studio to do the work the way our engineering teams would have: production-grade systems with eval harnesses, observability, version control, and runbooks. Built once. Owned by you. Designed to outlive any single vendor.

Today we work with operators across 7 industries — from dental clinics to SaaS — building voice agents, automation hubs, RAG systems, and reporting pipelines that quietly run their business in the background.

[ CORE VALUES ]

The four we run on.

01

Production over prototype

Anything we ship has retry logic, observability, alerts, and runbooks. If it can't survive Monday morning, we haven't shipped it.

02

You own it

Code in your repo, infra in your cloud, vendors you choose. We don't trap you in our platform. We don't have a platform.

03

Evidence over hype

We measure latency, deflection, recovery, hours saved. If the metric doesn't move, the system isn't worth running.

04

Small, deep, long

We take fewer clients. We go deeper. We stay longer. Most retainers run 12+ months because we keep finding wins.
