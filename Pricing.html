[ COMMON QUESTIONS ]

Before you commit.

Why is there a setup fee?+
The setup fee buys the build. Most AI agencies bake the cost into a high monthly that you keep paying forever even after the work is done. We don't — you pay for what we build, and the retainer is optional and proportionate.
Do I have to take a retainer?+
No. About 20% of clients walk after the build, run the system themselves, and check in only occasionally. The systems are documented and version-controlled. The retainer exists because most clients want continuous improvement, not because the system requires it.
What about model API costs?+
Pass-through. You bring the OpenAI / Anthropic / etc keys, you pay the bill directly. We model these in the audit so there are no surprises. For most clients model spend is <$300/mo per system.
Do you charge per seat?+
No. The systems we build are deployed on your infrastructure. There's no per-seat license fee from us, ever.
Can we start smaller?+
Yes. The Foundation tier ($8k) is one focused system. Many clients start there to validate the working relationship before committing to a multi-system sprint.
What if the system doesn't work?+
We define success criteria with you upfront (latency, deflection rate, hours saved). If we don't hit them, we keep working until we do, or refund the difference. We've never had to refund — but it's in writing.
